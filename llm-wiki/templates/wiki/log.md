# Operation log

The log is split by month under `log/`. Do not add entries to this file. Add each entry at the top of `log/YYYY-MM.md` for its month, newest first. Format: `## YYYY-MM-DD <Ingest|Query|Lint|Setup> <summary>`. When a month starts, create its file and add a link to it at the top of this list.
